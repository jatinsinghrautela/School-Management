# Incident response and recovery runbook

This runbook provides an operational starting point. The platform owner must name an incident lead and school contacts before a real-data pilot. Contact lists, jurisdiction-specific notification duties and deployment access belong in a private school-controlled record, not Git.

1. **Assess and contain.** Record the detection time, affected schools, symptoms and last known good operation. Stop affected writes/API workers when integrity is uncertain. Keep database and file access restricted to the incident team. Avoid using a privileged support session to experiment on real school records.
2. **Preserve evidence.** Preserve relevant audit history, infrastructure logs and an encrypted offline snapshot where appropriate. Record actions taken and their times. Never paste pupil data, passwords, session tokens or raw backups into public issues or chat. Retain the original affected instance offline rather than overwriting it during recovery.
3. **Address access.** Identify which credentials or sessions may have been exposed; revoke affected access and rotate infrastructure credentials through the authorized operator. Verify the impact across independent schools and organizations. Determine notification requirements with the school's responsible contacts.
4. **Recover separately.** Follow BACKUP-RECOVERY.md to authenticate a selected backup and restore into a new isolated target. Compare the backup time with the incident timeline. All restored session/reset tokens are revoked. Preserve evidence of subsequent activity that will not be present in the selected backup.
5. **Validate before service.** Verify school isolation, role/class memberships, login, notices, attendance, fees, published documents and upload scanning on the recovered instance. Check real SMTP/scanner configuration, infrastructure permissions, HTTPS and monitoring. Record integrity-check results and operator acceptance. Failed or unverified targets remain offline.
6. **Resume and review.** The incident lead authorizes manual cutover after acceptance. Monitor errors and reported discrepancies, record any data gaps, and communicate through the school's agreed channel. Produce a private incident report with impact, actions, recovery times, causes and follow-up fixes. Review the runbook and repeat the synthetic drill after relevant changes.

No automatic cutover, notification, deletion or external transmission is performed by this runbook. Actual-host disaster recovery, privacy/export/deletion handling and school acceptance remain release gates.
